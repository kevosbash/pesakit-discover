PK PESAKIT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The one-line version: everything stays on your phone. PesaKit has no servers, no account, and uploads nothing about you.

PesaKit turns the payment notification messages you already receive (M-Pesa and bank SMS) into a private cash-management dashboard. This policy explains exactly what the app accesses and what it does — and does not — do with it.

What PesaKit accesses

DataWhyWhere it goes
The text of payment SMS you approve, scan, or paste To read the amount, party, fee, balance, date and reference and show them on your dashboard Parsed and stored only in the app's private on-device database
Nothing else——

PesaKit does not read your contacts, your location, your other (non-payment) messages, your call logs, or any account credentials.

How messages reach PesaKit

  • One-tap capture (SMS User Consent). When a new payment SMS arrives, your phone's own system dialog asks whether PesaKit may read that single message. If you tap Allow, we receive only that one message. We never gain access to your inbox this way.
  • Paste or share. You copy a message, or share it into PesaKit from your Messages app. Nothing automated.
  • Full inbox scan (optional). With your explicit permission, PesaKit can scan payment messages already in your inbox from known financial senders (M-Pesa, banks) to build your history. It is off by default, opt-in, read-only, limited to those senders, processed on your device, and you can revoke SMS access anytime in Android Settings.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not transmit your messages or transactions to any server — ours or a third party's. Your financial data stays on your device. (The Discover tab downloads advert content — images and text only — but sends nothing about you; everything else works offline.)
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize your data.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs that profile you.
  • We do not retain anything after you delete it.

Retention and deletion

Your data lives only in PesaKit's private app storage. You are always in control:

  • Settings → Clear all data permanently deletes every stored transaction.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

There is no cloud copy to request deletion of, because there is no cloud copy.
